Two pages side by side

⇧⌘D splits the view; a tab or a favorite dragged onto one half of the page opens there. The address bar follows the pane you chose, and the session brings the pair back.

Zen mode

⇧⌘Z: the page, alone, edge to edge. The title bar comes back when the pointer goes looking for it, and the exit waits in the corner with the zoom slider.

Blocking, chosen and compiled

Wuji ships no list. The catalog is uBlock Origin's; “Recommended selection” installs nine in one click, and WebKit applies them in its network process — a blocked request never leaves. What WebKit can't do, Wuji replays with uBlock Origin's own code.

All about blocking

Why “Wuji”

無極, wújí: a Taoist term that literally means “summit of nothingness”. It is the endless source to which everything returns, tied to the perpetual movement of return, fu, 復. A browser that steps aside for the page bears its name.

What the project refuses to do

What's missing is decided, and each absence has its reason.

  • No dead controls

    A setting that controls nothing gives the illusion of a more advanced product than it is.

  • No telemetry

    The only request Wuji makes for itself checks the version — on a click, without any identifier.

  • No list chosen for you

    A missing list can't be wrong on your behalf: nothing is blocked until you've decided.

  • No search engine suggestions

    Every keystroke would go to a third party. The palette searches only on your Mac.

  • No filling on its own

    A login only goes into the field you clicked, never into a frame from another origin.

  • Measured before it's written

    Every number on this site comes from a benchmark, a test or the code — not from a promise.
