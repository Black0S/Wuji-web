Performance

The engine is WebKit: a page is parsed and run at Safari's speed. What a browser can do on top is stay out of the way — and that's where there was everything to gain.

Tabs that sleep

Suspended by WebKit

A tab you're not looking at has its JavaScript and layout suspended: a page that burns a whole core drops to 3% in the background, and picks up where it was, without reloading.

Asleep after a delay

Fifteen minutes, thirty, one, two or four hours — or only when the Mac runs short. The tab gives back its memory without leaving the column, and hovering wakes it up before the click.

What doesn't sleep

A page playing sound or loading isn't idle, and a tab where you typed in a form is kept — your input isn't lost to sleep.

A memory budget

Beyond a third of the Mac's memory, tabs left for more than five minutes go to sleep without waiting, oldest first — before macOS slows everything down. The setting shows the measurement.

Under macOS pressure

When the system runs short of memory, tabs go to sleep and the in-memory resource cache is emptied; the disk cache stays.

Display and network

The screen's frame rate

Up to 120 frames per second on a display that shows them; 60 in all cases in Low Power Mode.

Pre-warmed connections

Name resolution and the handshake start while you type an address, and as soon as you press a link to a site you've already visited: about 150 ms less before the first byte.

A tab ahead

A new tab is prepared in advance, its process already launched: a page opened in a new tab shows up in 88 ms instead of 140.

Nothing in the page's way

The column updates in place instead of rebuilding on every signal, a certificate check is remembered per host, and what Wuji fetches for itself uses HTTP/3 right away, four connections per host at most.

What the benchmark says

Everything macOS attributes to Wuji

At thirty tabs, the app weighs 139 MB; its rendering processes weigh 850 — the price of WebKit, which Safari pays the same way. The benchmark adds everything up: rendering, network, GPU.

The criterion

A profile taken while a heavy page loads no longer shows any Wuji symbol beyond an isolated sample: what's left to gain is in the engine.
