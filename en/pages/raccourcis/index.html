⌘ acts on what contains, ⌥ on the space: ⌘]/$ goes to the next tab, ⌥]/$ to the next space. macOS adapts shortcuts to your keyboard — the key to the right of P shows “]” on a QWERTY keyboard and “$” on an AZERTY keyboard. Wuji's menus always show your Mac's exact key.
